\textbf{Justifications}\par
Publication grimblat\_8/22/16\_computational (DOI: 10.1002/chem.201601150) is judged as \textbf{not at risk} of unreliability propagation because it is a review article. Review articles generally discuss existing research rather than execute computational procedures such as the WJH protocol. Review status therefore provides a reason not to infer that the authors used the protocol merely because they cited it.

Publication grimblat\_8/22/16\_computational (DOI: 10.1002/chem.201601150) is judged as \textbf{not at risk} of unreliability propagation because all citations to the WJH protocol appear in the introduction. This suggests that the protocol is cited as background information rather than used to conduct the research. However, this inference is defeasible: we identified two counterexamples previously in which all citations appeared in the introduction, but the citation text indicated use of the protocol.
