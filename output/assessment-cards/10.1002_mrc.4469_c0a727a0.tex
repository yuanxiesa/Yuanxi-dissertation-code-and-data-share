\textbf{Justifications}\par
Publication pierens\_2016\_investigation (DOI: 10.1002/mrc.4469) is judged as \textbf{not at risk} of unreliability propagation because all citations to the WJH protocol appear in the introduction. This suggests that the protocol is cited as background information rather than used to conduct the research. However, this inference is defeasible: we identified two counterexamples previously in which all citations appeared in the introduction, but the citation text indicated use of the protocol.
