This inference is defeasible: further evidence could lead us to revise the classification.

This suggests that the protocol is cited as background information rather than used to conduct the research. However, this inference is defeasible: we identified two counterexamples previously in which all citations appeared in the introduction, but the citation text indicated use of the protocol.
